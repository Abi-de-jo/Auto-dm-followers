# Surface brief

Scope: `/login` authentication surface.
Mode: Operate.
Audience: Instagram creator-operators returning to check automated conversations, deliveries, and captured leads. Job: authenticate quickly, trust the connection method, and understand FreshDM's comment-to-conversation mechanism before entering the workspace.
Chosen direction: Signal Handoff — a quiet ivory sign-in surface paired with a midnight-violet editorial visualization of one signal moving from comment to private reply to captured lead.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Signing in is the handoff between the creator and the automation work that continued while they were away.
OWN-WORLD: Warm ivory authentication paper against the established midnight-violet signal world; Inter typography, hairline rules, restrained violet accents, and one animated path instead of cards, testimonials, or invented metrics.
STORY: The creator is welcomed back, chooses Google or email access, sees the safety basis, and reads the core product mechanism in one continuous trail.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A calm, high-contrast auth form occupies the ivory side; a large statement and comment-to-reply-to-lead signal path occupy the dark side. Mobile compresses the same mechanism into a readable three-step rail above the form.
FORM: Signal Handoff, rank 1 of 1, seed direct-premium-refinement.
FINISH: Authentication remains keyboard accessible, errors explain recovery, motion is non-essential, mobile preserves the mechanism, and illustrative workflow content is labeled.
